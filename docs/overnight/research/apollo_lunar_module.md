# Aerospace Research: Apollo Lunar Module (LM)

## 1. Overview & Historical Specifications
- **Spacecraft Identifier**: Apollo Lunar Module (LM), Grumman Aerospace Corporation (Bethpage, NY).
- **Missions Flown**: Apollo 9 through Apollo 17 (6 successful lunar surface landings: Apollo 11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 17; Apollo 13 served as lifeboat).
- **Physical Dimensions**:
  - Total Height: 7.04 m (23.1 ft) on deployed landing gear.
  - Landing Gear Diagonal Span: 9.40 m (30.8 ft) across footpads.
  - Width across octagonal flats: 4.22 m (13.8 ft).
  - Total Stack Wet Mass (Apollo 11): ~15,200 kg (~33,500 lb).
    - Descent Stage Wet Mass: ~10,334 kg.
    - Ascent Stage Wet Mass: ~4,700 kg.

## 2. Staged Subsystems & Components

### 2.1 Descent Stage
- **Structure**: Cross-beam octagonal welded aluminum alloy prism with four quadrant bays for propellant and equipment storage:
  - Quad 1: Modular Equipment Stowage Assembly (MESA) containing lunar surface TV camera, sample collection tools, and core drills.
  - Quad 2: Primary water storage tanks and environmental control canisters.
  - Quad 3: Scientific payload bay (Apollo Lunar Surface Experiments Package, ALSEP).
  - Quad 4: Gaseous and supercritical helium pressurization tanks.
- **Thermal Multi-Layer Insulation (MLI)**:
  - Clad in 25-layer blankets of aluminized Kapton (golden amber tint) and aluminized Mylar (crinkled silver foil), suspended on fiberglass standoff studs.
  - Nickel alloy (Inconel) heat radiation shields around the descent engine cavity to withstand 1,100°C engine radiative plumes.
- **Descent Propulsion System (DPS)**:
  - TRW throttleable rocket engine (first deep-throttling human-rated rocket engine: 10% to 100% continuous thrust control).
  - Throttle Range: 4.4 kN to 43.9 kN (990 lbf to 9,870 lbf).
  - Specific Impulse ($I_{sp}$): 311 s (vacuum).
  - Propellant: Aerozine 50 (50/50 blend of hydrazine and UDMH) and Nitrogen Tetroxide (NTO), pressure-fed with supercritical helium.
  - Gimbal: Electric screw-jack actuators providing $\pm 6^\circ$ thrust vectoring through the vehicle's dynamic center of mass.
- **Landing Gear & Footpads**:
  - 4 cantilevered aluminum tubular legs with central shock-absorbing strut containing crushable aluminum honeycomb cartridges.
  - 4 circular dish-shaped aluminum footpads (0.94 m diameter) with downward-pointing 1.7 m (5.6 ft) lunar surface contact sensing probes ("Contact Light" indicator on instrument panel).
  - Forward leg equipped with 9-rung astronaut egress ladder reaching from the forward hatch porch to within 0.75 m of lunar regolith.

### 2.2 Ascent Stage
- **Crew Cabin**:
  - Pressurized cylinder (1.27 m diameter x 2.34 m length) holding 2 astronauts in a stand-up restraint harness (no heavy seats; saving ~120 kg).
  - 2 triangular forward windows (0.09 m² each) canted downward at 25° for direct landing site visualization.
  - Overhead docking window for alignment with the Command/Service Module (CSM).
- **Ascent Propulsion System (APS)**:
  - Bell Aerosystems fixed-thrust pressure-fed engine.
  - Thrust: 15.6 kN (3,500 lbf) constant vacuum thrust.
  - Specific Impulse ($I_{sp}$): 311 s.
  - Hypergolic propellants (Aerozine 50 / NTO) providing infallible single-chance lunar liftoff without ignition spark plugs.
- **Reaction Control System (RCS)**:
  - 4 external Marquardt R-4D quad clusters (16 thrusters total), each delivering 440 N (100 lbf) thrust in orthogonal couples for precision 3-axis attitude and translation control.
- **Communications & Radar**:
  - Steerable high-gain parabolic S-band dish antenna (0.66 m diameter) mounted atop the ascent stage.
  - Steerable Rendezvous Radar antenna mounted on the forward roof for tracking the orbiting CSM.

## 3. Materials & Visual Verification Standards
1. **Descent Octagon Foil**: Crinkled gold-amber Kapton foil (metalness 0.88, roughness 0.22) with black and silver thermal patchwork.
2. **Ascent Upper Shell**: Matte white and grey painted Al-Li sheets (metalness 0.25, roughness 0.40) with flush flush-riveted skin seams.
3. **Ascent Roof & Inconel Panels**: Dark gunmetal thermal shields (metalness 0.70, roughness 0.35).
4. **Leg Honeycomb Struts**: Clean anodized aluminum (metalness 0.80, roughness 0.20) wrapped in amber foil boots.
