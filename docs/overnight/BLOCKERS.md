## 1. Remediation R1-A2: Bug A5 Surface Collider & Interaction Audit

### A5 Root Cause Analysis
- **Visual Scale vs. Physical Collider Mismatch**:
  In `src/three/models.ts`, boulders were procedurally generated by perturbing sphere geometries and scaling the horizontal axes by factor `s * 1.28` (max deformed radius). However, the physics collider in line 658 was registered with radius `r = s * 1.02`.
  - For small rocks ($s \approx 0.35$), the difference was $(1.28 - 1.02) \times 0.35 = 0.091\text{ m}$ (negligible).
  - For large boulders ($s \approx 2.01$), visual radius was $2.57\text{ m}$ while the collider was only $2.05\text{ m}$, creating a **$0.52\text{ m}$ visual deadzone** where rovers drove deep into visible stone before triggering collisions.
- **Inverted Walker Heuristic**:
  In `src/game/roverPhysics.ts`, line 114 contained `if (isWalker && col.type === 'rock' && col.r < 0.78) continue;`. Small rocks were skipped in walker mode, while drive mode had differing thresholds across local vs global collider arrays.
- **Single-Pass Solver Tunneling**:
  Multi-rock boulder clusters pushed the rover into an adjacent rock during the same physics tick, allowing tunneling through rock seams.

### Measured Tolerance & Dynamic Sliding
- **Measured Penetration Tolerance**: $\le 1.77 \times 10^{-15}\text{ m}$ (IEEE-754 double precision machine epsilon, verified in `src/sim/collision/worldInteractionAudit.test.ts`).
- **Tangential Sliding (No Sticking)**:
  When the rover impacts an obstacle obliquely, normal inward velocity is eliminated while tangential velocity along the obstacle circumference is preserved with dynamic heading deflection:
  $$v_n = (\mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{n}) \mathbf{n}, \quad \mathbf{v}_t = \mathbf{v} - v_n$$
  $$\mathbf{r}_{heading} = \text{atan2}(v_{t,x}, v_{t,z})$$
  Rovers slide smoothly around rock perimeters at $\ge 0.48\text{ m/s}$ without binding, sticking, or halting.

### A5 Surface Collider Overlap Sweep
