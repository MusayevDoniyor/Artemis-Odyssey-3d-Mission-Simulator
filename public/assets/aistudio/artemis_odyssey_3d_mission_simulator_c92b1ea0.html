ARTEMIS SOUTH POLE REGION TARGET ZONE

NASA Artemis focuses on the lunar South Pole because of permanently shadowed craters (PSRs) that act as cold traps for volatile water ice, alongside elevated connecting ridges with nearly continuous solar illumination.

PLANETARY SCALE & ESCAPE PARADIGM

Leaving Earth requires overcoming an escape velocity of 11.19 km/s through a dense atmosphere ($101.3\text{ kPa}$). By contrast, launching off the Moon requires only 2.38 km/s in vacuum, which is why an Artemis Moon Base serves as humanity's stepping stone to Mars and the outer Solar System.

1. UNIVERSAL GRAVITATION (Newton)
F = G · (m₁ · m₂) / r²

On the Moon ($M = 7.342\times 10^{22}\text{ kg}, R = 1,737.4\text{ km}$), surface gravity calculates to precisely 1.625 m/s².

2. ROCKETRY THRUST & ACCELERATION
a = (Thrust · cos θ / Mass) - g_moon

Every kg of burned propellant decreases total vehicle mass, continuously increasing the Thrust-to-Weight Ratio (TWR) during descent.

ABOUT ARTEMIS ODYSSEY

NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026

Artemis Odyssey is an interactive 3D aerospace mission simulator and junior astronaut trainer built with accurate NASA specifications.

CORE EDUCATIONAL MISSIONS:
  • SLS Block 1 Launch: 4 RS-25 engines + 2 solid rocket boosters delivering 39.1 MN of liftoff thrust.
  • Lunar Landing: Powered descent and throttling equations toward the South Pole.
  • VIPER Rover: 4-wheel mobility and 1-meter TRIDENT ice-prospecting drill.
  • Outpost Management: Balancing life support, oxygen, and solar arrays.

Designed for young explorers aged 7–12 to experience real physics: $F = m \cdot a$, gravitational deceleration, and multi-stage rocket flight.
